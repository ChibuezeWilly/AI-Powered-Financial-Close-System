# FIN-003: Refund Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-003
**Version:** 1.2
**Effective Date:** 2025-03-01
**Owner:** Finance Department — Finance Manager

## Scope
Governs all customer refund processing at Northstar Technologies.

## Rules
1. All refunds require Finance Manager approval.
2. Refunds exceeding $5,000 require Controller approval.
3. Refunds must be linked to the original payment and invoice.
4. Refund reason must be documented before processing.
5. Refunds must be processed within 10 business days of approval.
6. Refunds to a different payment method than the original require additional verification.

## Thresholds
| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| $0 – $5,000 | Finance Manager |
| > $5,000 | Controller |

## Audit Requirements
- Full audit trail including original payment, reason, approval, and processing confirmation.
- Monthly refund summary in close report.
