# FIN-007: Write-Off Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-007
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller

## Scope
Governs the write-off of uncollectable receivables and other balance adjustments.

## Rules
1. Write-offs below $500 require Finance Manager approval.
2. Write-offs between $500 and $5,000 require Controller approval.
3. Write-offs exceeding $5,000 require CFO approval.
4. All write-offs must be preceded by documented collection efforts.
5. Write-offs must be recorded as bad debt expense.
6. Written-off amounts may be reversed if subsequently collected.

## Audit Requirements
- Quarterly write-off review by Controller.
- Annual external audit of all write-offs exceeding $1,000.
