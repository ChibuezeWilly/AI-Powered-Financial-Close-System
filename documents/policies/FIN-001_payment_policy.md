# FIN-001: Payment Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-001
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller
**Last Review:** 2026-06-01

## Scope

This policy governs the processing, recording, and reconciliation of all incoming customer payments and outgoing vendor payments for Northstar Technologies.

## Definitions

- **Payment**: Any transfer of funds received from a customer or sent to a vendor.
- **Payment Allocation**: The process of matching a received payment to one or more outstanding invoices.
- **Unapplied Cash**: Payment received that cannot be matched to a specific invoice.
- **Payment Terms**: The agreed timeframe within which payment must be received (e.g., NET15, NET30, NET45).

## Rules

1. All payments must be recorded on the date they are received or sent.
2. Payments must be allocated to invoices within 3 business days of receipt.
3. Payments exceeding the invoice amount by more than $50 must be investigated before allocation.
4. Partial payments must be recorded against the corresponding invoice with the remaining balance maintained.
5. Payments received in a currency different from the invoice currency must be converted using the daily closing exchange rate from an approved FX provider.
6. Duplicate payments must be flagged immediately and held in a suspense account pending investigation.
7. Any payment older than 90 days without allocation requires escalation to the Finance Manager.

## Thresholds

| Action | Threshold | Approver |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Standard payment allocation | Any amount | Finance Analyst |
| Partial payment acceptance | < $1,000 variance | Finance Analyst |
| Partial payment acceptance | ≥ $1,000 variance | Finance Manager |
| Payment write-off | < $500 | Finance Manager |
| Payment write-off | ≥ $500 | Controller |
| Refund processing | Any amount | Finance Manager |

## Required Approvals

- All refunds require Finance Manager approval regardless of amount.
- Payment write-offs above $500 require Controller sign-off.
- Cross-currency payment adjustments exceeding $200 FX difference require Finance Manager review.

## Exceptions

Payment processing may be expedited in the following cases:
- End of quarter close (approval may be delegated to Senior Analyst)
- Customer credit hold situations
- Legal hold requirements

## Escalation

1. Unmatched payments after 5 business days → Finance Manager notification
2. Unmatched payments after 15 business days → Controller review
3. Suspected duplicate payments → Immediate Finance Manager alert
4. Payments from unknown entities → Compliance review

## Audit Requirements

- All payment allocations must be logged with timestamp, user, and invoice reference.
- Payment modifications require before/after state recording.
- Monthly payment reconciliation report is mandatory.
- Quarterly external audit sampling of 10% of transactions.

## Examples

**Example 1: Standard Payment**
Customer Acme Inc pays $12,400 against INV-1001 for $12,400. Payment is recorded, allocated, and invoice marked as paid.

**Example 2: Partial Payment**
Customer pays $10,000 against INV-1001 for $12,400. The $10,000 is allocated, and the $2,400 balance remains on the invoice. Investigation may be triggered depending on the customer's payment history.

**Example 3: Overpayment**
Customer pays $13,000 against INV-1001 for $12,400. The $600 excess is held in unapplied cash. Finance Analyst contacts the customer to determine if the overpayment should be applied to another invoice or refunded.
