# FIN-013: Exception Management Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-013
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller

## Scope
Governs the handling of financial exceptions, discrepancies, and anomalies detected during reconciliation.

## Rules
1. All discrepancies above $100 must have an investigation record.
2. Critical severity discrepancies must be investigated within 24 hours.
3. High severity discrepancies must be investigated within 3 business days.
4. Medium severity discrepancies must be investigated within 5 business days.
5. AI-generated root causes must be clearly labeled as hypotheses, not facts.
6. Human approval is required before any accounting modification.
7. Every investigation must preserve the evidence chain.
8. Failed investigations must be escalated, not silently closed.

## Severity Classification
| Severity | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| LOW | Difference < $100, timing difference |
| MEDIUM | Difference $100–$999, partial payment |
| HIGH | Difference ≥ $1,000, unauthorized discount |
| CRITICAL | Difference ≥ $5,000, duplicate payment, fraud indicators |

## Escalation Timeline
1. Unresolved after 5 days → Finance Manager notification
2. Unresolved after 10 days → Controller review
3. Unresolved after 15 days → CFO escalation
4. Suspected fraud → Immediate compliance alert
