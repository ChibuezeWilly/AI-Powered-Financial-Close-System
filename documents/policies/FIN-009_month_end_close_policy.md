# FIN-009: Month-End Close Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-009
**Version:** 1.5
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller

## Scope
Governs the monthly financial close process at Northstar Technologies.

## Rules
1. Only administrators and controllers may close an accounting period.
2. All critical discrepancies must be resolved or explicitly escalated before close.
3. A monthly close report must be generated before the period is closed.
4. Closing a period prevents new transactions from being posted.
5. Reopening a closed period requires Controller approval with documented reason.
6. The close process must verify all reconciliations are complete.
7. Outstanding discrepancies must be documented in the close report.

## Close Checklist
1. Run final reconciliation
2. Verify all high/critical discrepancies are resolved
3. Review and approve all pending adjustments
4. Generate monthly close report
5. Review report with Controller
6. Close period
7. Archive supporting documentation

## Reopen Rules
- Reopening requires Controller approval
- Reason must be documented
- All changes during the reopened period are audit-logged
- Period must be re-closed after corrections

## Audit Requirements
- Close and reopen events are logged with user, timestamp, and reason.
- Monthly close report is immutable once period is closed.
