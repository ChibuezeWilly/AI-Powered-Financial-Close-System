# FIN-005: Journal Entry Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-005
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller

## Scope
Governs the creation, approval, and posting of journal entries at Northstar Technologies.

## Rules
1. Every journal entry must balance (total debits = total credits).
2. Journal entries must include a description explaining the business purpose.
3. Journal entries must reference the source transaction or supporting documentation.
4. Adjusting journal entries require Finance Manager approval.
5. Journal entries exceeding $10,000 require Controller approval.
6. Journal entries cannot be posted to closed accounting periods without reopening.
7. Reversing journal entries must reference the original entry.
8. Recurring journal entries must be reviewed quarterly.

## Thresholds
| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| $0 – $2,500 | Finance Analyst |
| $2,501 – $10,000 | Finance Manager |
| > $10,000 | Controller |

## Audit Requirements
- All journal entries logged with full before/after state.
- Monthly journal entry review during close.
- Quarterly audit of manual journal entries.
