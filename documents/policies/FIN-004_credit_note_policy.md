# FIN-004: Credit Note Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-004
**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** 2025-01-15
**Owner:** Finance Department — Controller

## Scope
Governs the issuance, recording, and reconciliation of credit notes at Northstar Technologies.

## Rules
1. Credit notes must reference the original invoice.
2. Credit notes exceeding $1,000 require Finance Manager approval.
3. Credit notes exceeding $5,000 require Controller approval.
4. Credit notes must be issued within the same accounting period as the triggering event when possible.
5. Credit notes applied to closed periods require period reopening with Controller approval.
6. All credit notes must include: customer name, original invoice ID, credit amount, reason, and issuer.

## Thresholds
| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| $0 – $1,000 | Finance Analyst |
| $1,001 – $5,000 | Finance Manager |
| > $5,000 | Controller |

## Audit Requirements
- Credit notes are included in monthly reconciliation.
- Quarterly audit of all credit notes exceeding $2,500.
